# PRODUCT CONTEXT: KeuanganKu

## Purpose
KeuanganKu is a modern, responsive multi-user personal finance platform that allows users to seamlessly track income and expenses, visualize their cash flow trends, and get precise visual categorization.

## Key Features
- **Authentication**: JWT-based session security (Login/Register).
- **Transaction Manager**: Multi-user CRUD ledger (Income vs Expense with custom categories, dates, and amounts).
- **Fintech Dashboard**: Balance counters, total stats, Recharts visualizations (Monthly bar chart trend and category breakdowns), and search/filtering controls.

## Target Experience: Premium Fintech (Revolut/Stripe)
- Minimalist, high-end styling with micro-details.
- Sophisticated color palette: deep indigo, emerald, rose, and pristine whites/slates.
- Dynamic responsive grids, interactive modals, and subtle transitions.
